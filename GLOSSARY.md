# Glossary

**Account**: A person's registered identity in Mira. Required to use any planning feature. Has one Username, one email, and one password.

**Username**: The unique public name an Account signs in with. Chosen at registration; cannot collide with another Account's Username.

**Email**: Contact address attached to an Account at registration. Not used to sign in.

**Visitor**: Someone without an Account, or not signed in. Can see the landing page only.

**Plan**: The meal-and-move schedule generated for an Account from its Profile.

**Profile**: The body and goal details (sex, activity, goal, etc.) entered in the planning wizard that a Plan is built from.

**Plan day**: One of the 7 scheduled days of a Plan (meals plus a workout), shown on a calendar date. A Plan repeats every 7 days.

**Log**: The record of which Plan items an Account has ticked off, and water drunk, per day.

**Account data**: The Profile, Plan, Log, and Custom Meals and Moves saved for one Account. It follows the Account across devices.

**Meal**: One food menu item (name, meal slot, calories, macros, diet). Either Built-in or Custom.

**Move**: One exercise (name, kind, intensity, minutes). Either Built-in or Custom. "Exercise" and "latihan" mean Move.

**Built-in**: A Meal or Move that ships with Mira. Every Account sees the same ones. Nobody can edit, hide, or delete them.

**Custom**: A Meal or Move an Account made itself. Only that Account sees it. The Account can edit or delete it any time. Part of Account data.

**Library**: The place where an Account sees every Meal and Move it can use: all Built-in ones plus its own Custom ones. Custom ones are added, edited and deleted here.

**Remake**: Building a fresh Plan from an edited Profile. Drops all Plan day edits and the Log from today onward. Past Log stays.
