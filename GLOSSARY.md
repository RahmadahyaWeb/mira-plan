# Glossary

**Account**: A person's registered identity in Mira. Required to use any planning feature. Has one Username, one email, and one password.

**Username**: The unique public name an Account signs in with. Chosen at registration; cannot collide with another Account's Username.

**Email**: Contact address attached to an Account at registration. Not used to sign in.

**Visitor**: Someone without an Account, or not signed in. Can see the landing page only.

**Plan**: The meal-and-move schedule generated for an Account from its Profile.

**Profile**: The body and goal details (sex, activity, goal, etc.) entered in the planning wizard that a Plan is built from.

**Log**: The record of which Plan items an Account has ticked off, and water drunk, per day.

**Account data**: The Profile, Plan and Log saved for one Account. It follows the Account across devices.
